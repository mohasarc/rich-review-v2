Read alongside the six findings in [critique.md](critique.md): **A** stopping layer, **B** reason attribution, **C** representation, **D** human judgment, **E** evidence reach, **F** learning evidence. These letters are topics, not grades. Row order is experiment order, not a ranking.

Rows 01–37 draw on the experiment README and a browser-rendered opening/decision-surface scan. For the written critique, 38, I read its entire README, critique, and ledger after it finished. Further mechanism, source, method, and screenshot reading was targeted. Numbers describing inventories or probe suites belong to the producing experiment; they are not independent completeness or correctness findings from this pass. I did not traverse every interactive state or read every duplicated source snapshot. [Reading captures](reading-captures/) retain rendered text, headings, and controls, including hidden authored text where already present in the DOM. They are receipts for the available reading surface, not proof of comprehension or an exhaustive UI audit.
