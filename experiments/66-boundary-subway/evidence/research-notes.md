## Real versus illustrative

The graph is curated from source, not extracted automatically from a trace. Edges represent selected transfers of responsibility and sometimes omit intermediate transport/collaborator calls. Journey order is an explanatory source walk; no request, worker, queue, socket or clock runs in the browser. Sizes count the distinct flow types assigned to a grouped stop, not observed traffic, cost or importance. Authority roles are editorial classifications.

ELK computes every station position, compound enclosure and route bend. For readable geometry, backward semantic edges are oriented by declared responsibility stages for layout and retain a reversal bit for directional chevrons. This is a layout constraint, not a changed runtime direction. ELK also produces the alternate phone layouts. D3 supplies joins, symbols, paths, zoom and camera transitions. No hand-written graph layout, routing algorithm, semantic zoom or animation loop is used.
