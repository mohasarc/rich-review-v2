The final three commits return to package-test concerns: portable hashes and Windows cleanup. This is why chronology alone does not make a clean split.

Why stop at four?

Time and authority can change independently inside the existing CLI. Package staging can then be exercised through built entries without a client. Routing, output ownership and lifecycle control stay together because they form the new host contract. The test moves and removed checks stay attached to staging, where their meaning changes.

The future readiness/completion lifetime changes remain recorded deferrals inside cut 01. They are not implementations delivered by this PR. The production consumer switch remains outside this subject.
