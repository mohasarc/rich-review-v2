05
REASON STATED
The first routing decision stops later observation

Disabled execution bypasses registry routing. Otherwise evaluate record present → not starting → version compatible → responsive, in that order. Read/observe lazily and memoize each operation. Missing, starting, recovering, dead, incompatible, and warm routes keep their distinct effects; only confirmed exit asks routing to remove the record.

WHY RECORDED

The first routing decision must prevent every later side effect.

06
REASON STATED
Answer locally without waiting for warm-up

Cold/fallback attempts create a fresh host executor. Absent records and fallback routes trigger startup independently; starting/recovering routes do not. Swallow background trigger failures, preserve the chosen route, forward argv/cwd/telemetry, and give local executors a no-op resource sampler.

WHY RECORDED

Ordinary commands must answer locally while a daemon warms; a daemon becoming ready must not cancel or duplicate local work.

07
REASON STATED
Replay warm work only when transport says it is safe

Use the existing DaemonTransportError.retrySafe authority for one local fallback. Uncertain submission, accepted-work failure, non-retry-safe rejection, and malformed/failed completion stay warm and return a controlled failure; they do not replay locally or invalidate ownership through this client path.

WHY RECORDED

Timeout or disconnect is not evidence that a healthy daemon or accepted work can be discarded.
