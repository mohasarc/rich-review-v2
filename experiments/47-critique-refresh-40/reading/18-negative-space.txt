OWNERSHIP MOVES
Warm capture, cleanup, and failure output.

The client creates the capture; the host does not provide a storage or controlled-output factory. Malformed output is disposed where available; missing output becomes a controlled result. The completion spool gets a local record codec, removing its import of the CLI output helper.

RETRY AUTHORITY STAYS
Only an explicit safe failure permits fallback.

Accepted or ambiguous work does not become a fresh local command. Workspace-capacity and response-capacity failures retain their specific messages; other failures use the accepted-request message.

Stated reason

Warm-transfer cleanup and replay safety belong to the daemon client. Moving the owner carries the existing execution contract with it.
