Recovery scopes
Numeric counters replace fixed recovery control flow: 1 execution reattachment per call; 1 fetch-resume allowance inside each execute attempt. A failed fetch feeds completion failure; a reattachment gets a fresh allowance.
Stated
PR: each reattached execute attempt needs its own fetch-resume allowance.

07
Output + framing
Capture, spool, disk decoding, socket codec, and both worker-side checks share the output slice: 64 KiB chunks / 256 KiB inline / 256 MiB result / 512 MiB aggregate. Control caps stay 8 MiB JSON / 256 KiB execution control.
Stated
PR + policy record: require the slice throughout the bounded output path.

Memory + supervision
Delete DaemonResourcePolicy and the second memory-cap input. Use the snapshot’s RSS / worker-heap thresholds for supervision, launch, and reporting; retain 250 ms supervision / 25 ms heap samples / 2 replacements in 10 min, then drain.
Stated
Policy record: one derivation, resource shedding before replacement, and a bounded replacement circuit.

09
Startup + shutdown
Required slices preserve coordination, heartbeats, polls, stop escalation, idle lifetime, and drain acknowledgement timings. Startup child-failure retry becomes numeric (default 1). The unused healthy-startup timeout option disappears; healthy warm-up remains unbounded.
Stated
Policy record: preserve distinct cadences and deadlines; progressing warm-up has no project-size deadline.

Diagnostics
