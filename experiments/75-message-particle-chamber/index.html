THE COMPLETE SURFACE

Seven facts. One answer.

Everything taught by the controls and receipts is named here. This is the stopping layer for the selected delivery mechanism.

  1. 01

    A 4-byte length prefix gates a whole frame; its high bit marks binary. Split bytes wait, coalesced frames separate. Malformed, truncated, oversized and checksum-corrupt inputs fail. JSON, execution-control and raw-chunk limits are separate.

    UNEXPLAINED Exact format and caps are inherited; no original rationale found.Receipt ↗
  2. 02

    Each connection needs the same manifest. Request and transfer IDs must match; offset and sequence equal the next record ordinal, advanced after append resolves. Matching end counts and a whole-result digest precede usable output.

    UNEXPLAINED These retained validation choices have no specific reason in the inspected prose.Receipt ↗
  3. 03

    After a manifest, fetch from the next record. An authenticated accepted close can instead reattach with the identical execute request; matching ledger identity reuses work, conflicting payload rejects. Current limits: 1 fetch per attempt; 1 accepted reattachment.

    STATED / UNEXPLAINED Replay safety belongs to the client; the exact inherited limits have no stated reason.Receipt ↗
  4. 04

    A lost accepted or submitted-unconfirmed response gives no local replay permission. Not-submitted failures and eligible authenticated admission rejections can be retry-safe. Exhausted accepted recovery yields a controlled warm failure.

    STATED The package owns replay safety and controlled failures.Receipt ↗
  5. 05

    The package creates partial capture. Validate → acknowledge → resolve transfers usable output to the caller. Invalid or abandoned capture is disposed; ack failure disposes finished output. Server ack checks transfer identity and cleans the spool; cleanup errors are recorded.

    STATED / UNEXPLAINED Client cleanup ownership is stated; exact ack ordering and cleanup-error policy are unexplained.Receipt ↗
  6. 06

    Only disconnected diagnostic traces expire after 5 minutes, or earlier over the 1,024-trace cap. Expiry retains spool and ledger. Ack removes the spool and trace, but keeps the ledger entry. Ledger eviction is deferred in the follow-up spec.

    STATED / UNEXPLAINED Eviction is explicitly deferred; current trace limits and retained bookkeeping have no original rationale here.Receipt ↗
