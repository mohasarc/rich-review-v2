# Experiment ledger

Each entry records something to preserve, a limitation or repeated pattern, and a discriminating next step. These are editorial judgments against the philosophy and playbook; they are not symnav correctness scores. Counts and execution claims below are attributed to the experiment's own report unless the [review notes](review-notes.md) identify a check performed in this critique.

Every README was read in full. Every delivered entry page was opened from disk and its opening/structure inspected; deeper content was sampled as recorded. Archived attempts are distinguished from delivered artifacts. [Return to the complete critique](critique.md).
