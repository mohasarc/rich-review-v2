## Limits of the critique

The main judgments about reading burden, useful compression, transfer, and attention are editorial inferences. No fresh human reader participated. The criteria for future trials are proposals, not empirical findings.

There is one targeted rationale cross-check, not an exhaustive independent semantic inventory of the entire stack. The critique deliberately does not claim that every other rationale label, decision inventory, or behavior interpretation is correct. The source-check example shows why such validation cannot be inferred from link integrity alone.
