## What I tried

The starting scope contained 01–79 and 81. Experiment 80 finished during the reading, so I added its complete handoff, ten reading records, staged/consolidated snapshots, and selected source readers before freezing the final census. Experiments 82 and 84 remained unfinished at that cutoff.

The critique recognizes that later visuals do explanatory work. It examines 79's distinct recovery loops, 80's class/visibility/package boundaries, and 78's fixed cleanup condition against 64's shared event number. It also identifies the unresolved work: composing complete stack understanding, supporting context-sensitive design judgment, carrying corrections into reused claims, and observing unfamiliar human learning.

I captured 80 HTML entries including companions, saved 50 operated states across 16 artifacts, and independently retrieved three source witnesses. These establish available behavior and specific factual distinctions; they do not establish comprehension. Bulk capture output was too unwieldy, so reading shifted to bounded excerpts and targeted full passages. One mistaken navigation selector was corrected and retained in the record. No page prototype or reader study was attempted.

[verification.json](verification.json) checks this handoff's structure, local links, capture availability, operation counts, and clean supplied worktrees. It does not certify every campaign claim or reproduce the authors' Symnav test runs.

## What I would drop

The bulk DOM captures as a reading interface. They are useful receipts but expose too much at once, including off-camera text. The long ledger earns its place as coverage depth; it should not become the default route through the critique.

## What I would do next
