# What the pictures now let a reader work out

The collection now contains explanations in which a picture does real work: a caller can keep a promise after its cache forgets it; a return prevents later observations; a broken connection leaves an accepted request intact. These relationships are a substantial step toward the understanding described in [philosophy.md](../../philosophy.md). The unresolved problem is carrying them into an account of a whole change that a person can retain, question, and stop reading.

This refresh covers **81 finished handoffs: 01–81**, including 80, which finished during this reading. Earlier experiments were read. The [ledger](experiment-ledger.md) gives every handoff an individual reading, including the three interrupted negative results. [Inspection notes](inspection-notes.md) state the cutoff, reading extent, operated routes, and source checks. This is a cumulative, primed agent critique, not a fresh human reading or an audit of every possible UI state. No page was built.

The first four have a useful spatial expression: closed flippers, different return loops, a persistent identity, and visible capture state. The final case depends much more on the prose and classifier receipt. That is the form's boundary. Bumpers can express ordered refusal; a single ball cannot explain every concurrent failure fact or a many-to-one classification rule by motion alone.

79 handles that boundary reasonably. It identifies the server duplicate branch and local executor lane as source projections, and the observations as real methods with injected peers. Its physics does not choose the branch. I operated the delivered replay; I did not rerun those Symnav probes or observe a real daemon. The successful source-dialog return also retained the selected case and returned focus to its opener. [Return receipt](operations/79-recording-return.json).

This makes 79 a useful companion to [72's circuit](../72-admission-circuit-board/index.html), [73's recovery field](../73-daemon-weather-map/index.html), and [75's transfer chamber](../75-message-particle-chamber/index.html). ## What is still missing

### Completeness should live in the model the reader carries away

01 and 14 make the stack's owners visible, then ask the reader to carry extensive choice sheets. 21 explicitly makes its film an orientation and puts the complete inventory below it. 03, 06, 11, and 12 make the same compromise at PR scale. Their honesty matters: the opening is not quietly advertised as sufficient when a later register carries the obligations.

But the philosophy asks for a change that becomes smaller in the reader's head. A long root can satisfy rule 4 while missing that aim. Calling more material “the top” solves a disclosure problem; it does not necessarily solve an understanding problem.

The newer brief scopes must also be respected. [79's brief](../79-failure-domain-pinball/brief.md), like the recent representation briefs, explicitly permits a narrow lesson. 68 is not deficient because its routing microscope omits unrelated cache decisions. The campaign still needs a whole-stack explanation, but that is a **composition problem across these lessons**, not a reason to force every small instrument to contain the entire stack.

51 makes the most relevant structural attempt: nine fronts organize consequences, while original PR bullets become evidence underneath them. 59's audit shows why that organization still needs checking. A descendant can have a valid parent elsewhere in the complete overview; conversely, a mapped PR bullet can be present while a deeper policy-table consequence remains unannounced. A claim ID and a working link cannot establish semantic completeness. [59's parent analysis](../59-critique-response-59/parent-repairs.md).

76 offers a different building block: the same enclosure persists while selected contents reset. Its two specimens let a reader compare a cache turn with an execution attempt without asserting that their semantics are identical. This is closer to reusable explanation than copying a shared card component. A useful next composition would keep the common question—what survives this boundary—while leaving the different failure and authority rules visible.

### Decisions need a reason to matter to the human

The collection is strong on identifying owners and increasingly strong on showing consequences. It is weaker on turning those consequences into a design question informed by context.

70 is a helpful case. Its three consumers receive more policy fields than they read, and the registry retains a scalar method override. This makes a real choice available for discussion: how much authority and coupling should an input expose? A reader can weigh that only if the known reason, the unexplained part, and the conditions that would change the tradeoff stay separate. The diagram must not manufacture the missing answer.

11's better alternatives, 29's proposed review units, and 35's reconsideration cues supply useful precedents. Their weaker versions are generic alternatives or a prompt to “think about” something without a concrete consequence. A practical next reading task should ask for a prediction and then ask what missing context would make the reader challenge the choice. Agreement with the implementer is not a comprehension measure.

The philosophy's eventual responding reader does **not** justify adding verdicts or comment storage tonight. Rule 2 explicitly makes this artifact read-only. The missing observation can happen outside the teaching page.

### Coverage and correctness machinery do not establish understanding

The methods have valuable, distinct outputs: 17 exposes a controlled execution boundary; 27 distinguishes what a test actually reaches; 28 exposes a reference-query blind spot; 31 records extraction and parent assignments; 32 makes some authoring obligations mechanical. Those capabilities should retain their exact claims.

19 is especially instructive because it says plainly that a no-bodies method cannot meet the requirement to surface weakened tests and every implementation decision. Its type lesson remains useful, but its admitted blind area is not complete compliance with original rule 8. A declaration view needs another source of those decisions. In 20, the five #127 decision records cover callable behavior but do not give the added characterization tests their own decision account. A callable census and a changed-file census answer different questions. [19's explicit limit](reading/19-type-level-only--index.txt), [20's current opening](reading/20-contract-table--index.txt).

The same separation applies to learning evidence. 49's four independent agent sessions support a bounded result about two wording changes and prompted predictions. They do not observe human navigation or whole-page learning. 52's compact and fuller registers retain the same 2,305 words; reduced height is a layout result. Its reader slots remain empty. 56 is interrupted work with a partial protocol, not a failed comprehension study. The successive fresh-reader reports preserve useful misses and corrections, but their repeated subjects prime their readers. This critique has that limitation too.

The useful correction record is small: affected wording and location, revision, source witness, qualified replacement, and application status. It should not become a gold answer key or another scoring system. This critique supplies the distinctions and leaves the original experiments unchanged.

### Visual variety and editorial repetition coexist

62's glass, 67's terrain, 69's gesture, 74's loom, 77's films, 79's table, and 80's type specimen are meaningfully different instruments. Their surrounding reading structure is much more alike: large opening, notation key, interaction, a complete prose register, then receipts. That structure often keeps the awkward facts—test changes, public seams, and rationale gaps—outside the part the reader manipulates.

Those facts should not be removed to preserve the metaphor. They should be attached to the claim they qualify. 77 moves in that direction: selecting the test film changes the middle source station to an absence and names the changed worker-test observations beside it. A decorative test badge would not do that. [Operated test film](operations/77-test-film.png).

The notation also has a cost. 70 teaches seven rules before its three compound glyphs can be read fully. 67's terrain preserves measured summit counts but gives its intervening relief no measured meaning. 71 counts physical path churn with moves at both addresses; its skyline is not runtime cost or the supplied rename-aware PR stat. These are disclosed limits, not automatically false representations. The question is whether the reader can continue to use the correct unit after the legend is out of sight.

Several READMEs report that phone fit becomes a locator and normal-size text or magnification does the reading. That limits a claim that the visual itself replaces prose on that surface. My operated comparison is desktop only; the mobile limitations here are authors' reports.

## The next work should resolve one remaining uncertainty

**Carry the checked corrections into any reused claims.** Keep original stimuli intact and identify the corrected copy. Do not flatten a partial rationale into either “everything explained” or “nobody said anything.”

**Run one comparison around an already discriminating question.** 72 and 79 can support a shared acceptance-and-recovery question; 64 and 78 expose a comparison-condition problem that should be repaired before evaluating their forms. Freeze the same scoped facts and separate predictions from context-sensitive design reasoning. Keep literal reader explanations and the first descent that changes them. A human result remains missing; no trial is claimed here.

**Then try composition on a complete stack front.** Keep one of 51's consequence fronts, use a retained-identity relationship from 76, and attach its test and rationale qualifications to the affected boundary. The useful outcome would identify which facts that shared picture honestly carries and which still need another representation. Another inventory of every attractive donor would reproduce the current cost.
