## How findings are classified

A **source correction** concerns a claim contradicted by the inspected source, such as first-versus-last primary ownership. An **omission** concerns a required decision absent from an advertised complete layer or a particular reader’s account; those are distinguished rather than conflated. A **descent surprise** needs an identified parent and child and the exact newly revealed proposition. An **educational hypothesis** is a proposal about what might help a reader, not a measured result.

This distinction prevents three tempting mistakes: counting every missed fact as an R4 failure, taking an unvisited drawer’s existence as proof the top was complete, and calling all the cases without recorded surprises compliant. It also preserves disagreement: 39 and 42 use different classifications for 27’s fixture explanation. The critique states the shared practical issue and the narrower evidence each actually supplies.

The absence of comment storage and approve/reject controls is required by playbook rule 2. It is not a missing feature in tonight’s artifacts, even though the philosophy describes eventual reader response. Likewise, explicitly partial orientations such as 21’s film and 32’s starter are evaluated together with their linked complete artifacts.

## What changed in my own approach

The broad browser capture was useful for finding entry points, labels, repeated structures, and concrete claims. Its raw size was a poor critique: a control inventory or word count does not say what someone understood. I retained the captures as receipts and shifted the written result to a complete opening comparison, one ledger row per experiment, and a small set of falsifiable witnesses.

The earlier critiques had already made the general convergence diagnosis. I therefore concentrated this refresh on the later synthesis, newly finished reader reports, competing omission/surprise classifications, and direct attribution checks. No attempt is made to select a winner, assign scores, create a gold answer key, or generalize a measured learning effect from my primed reading.
