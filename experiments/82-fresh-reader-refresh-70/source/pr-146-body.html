107 ## Decisions
108 
109 - Chose one injected delivery session over process-shell delivery fields, because attachment, transfer, acknowledgement, and trace lifecycles form one coordination boundary.
110 - Chose narrow ledger and spool ports over moving their data into the session, because accepted-request state and completion bytes already have authoritative owners.
111 - Chose the latest per-request delivery promise over aggregating duplicate attachments, because queue completion must wait for the current stream without creating another request registry.
112 - Chose retention-fenced trace handles over raw observer handles, because evicted or expired traces must ignore late diagnostics.
113 - Chose physical cleanup before logical acknowledgement over conditional journal acknowledgement, because cleanup failure remains diagnostic and must not withhold protocol success.
