## 2. The change (my words, at most three sentences)

In symnav PR #131, three daemon classes that live in the CLI app (resource supervisor, process terminator, registry) stop getting their thresholds from CLI-local constants/defaults and instead must be handed a whole section (resources / shutdown / startup) of the daemon package's already-existing policy through their constructors; the numbers themselves (250 ms, 2 replacements per 10 min, 500 ms / 20 ms, 15 s) do not change and the classes stay in apps/cli. Tests are reshaped so small test thresholds come through validated policy overrides or test-only helpers (the terminator's numeric constructor moves into a test helper), seven CLI policy test cases are deleted and one new 17 ms supervisor case is added. One loophole remains: the registry method `startupOwnerIsWithinGrace` still accepts an optional numeric `graceMs` that overrides the policy value.

Boundary movement:
- **Value authority** moved across the package boundary: the numbers used to come from CLI-local threshold sources ("Local threshold sources are retired"); now they come only from the daemon package's policy. The **code** boundary did not move (the consumers stay in apps/cli).
- **Constructor boundary** changed from local/implicit numbers to a *required* policy-section parameter; "required" is a TypeScript type requirement only, with no validation in the constructors.
- **Test boundary**: production compatibility overloads are removed; the old numeric construction shape of the terminator survives only as a test helper; the registry test helper fills in the startup section.
- **Leak in the new boundary**: the registry method-level `graceMs` override remains, so a caller can still bypass the policy grace per call.
