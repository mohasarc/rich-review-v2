THE COMPLETE STOPPING LAYER
What the two pieces commit to.

Every decision explained below is named here.
Stated = a recorded reason. Gap = no specific reason found in the inspected PR prose and commits.

01
Fill the package; keep the old route. ↘

#148 stages the client and six grouped mechanism families. The shipped CLI still uses its own graph; 38 production copies are frozen.

STATEDSeparate mechanism ownership from host invocation coordination. — PR #148

02
Move execution, keep the host. ↘

#149 wires CLI invocations and lifecycle controls to DaemonClient, then removes the old mechanism directory. CLI keeps argv/workspace selection, local execution and its injected backend executor.

STATEDInvocation classification and workspace discovery are host responsibilities. — PR #149

03
A small opening on a large body. ↘

The public client has Node-free declarations and dynamically loads its runtime. The final four paths are root, process-entry, worker-entry and testing. Policy serialization becomes internal; the temporary policy-testing path is removed.

STATED + GAPNode-free host declarations and a closed export inventory are stated. The specific API removals have no separate rationale in the inspected PR prose.

04
The body owns its collaborators. ↘

Transport, process/lifecycle, worker/execution, capture/delivery, resources and registry/diagnostics sit behind the client. Result capture is package-owned; #149 replaces LocalDaemonTransport with focused composition.

STATEDWarm-output cleanup and replay safety belong to the client; split transport owners now share one package boundary. — PRs #148–149

05
Tests get a window, not the keys. ↘

External tests and benchmarks use a read-only testing inspector instead of private registry, diagnostic and spool paths. Controlled actors move to daemon and receive an executor URL.

STATEDExpose observations without storage/mutation authority; keep package tests independent of CLI build layout. — PR #149

06
Turn a promise into a wall. ↘

The freeze check becomes a physical-absence check. Compiler-backed inventories constrain CLI reachability, exports, clock sources and external storage access.

STATEDOrdinary TypeScript syntax must not bypass final ownership rules. — PR #149

07
Move the tests with their owner. ↘

#148 relocates 37 co-located mechanism test files. It also serializes daemon test files and adds tsx for development; those exact runner choices have no stated reason in the inspected prose.

STATED + GAPPackage ownership is stated. A migration reason alone does not explain fileParallelism: false or the specific dependency choice.

08
Clearing space also removes observations. ↘

#149 deletes seven built-CLI status scenarios and three stop scenarios, including prompt-status and in-flight-stop observations. Their replacement coverage is not established here.

UNEXPLAINEDThe PR explains public test boundaries, but gives no scenario-specific reason for these ten removals.
