## 2. The change, in at most three sentences

These are the last two PRs of a 26-PR stack. #148 fills `@symnav/daemon` with its own public client and grouped daemon mechanism families: transport, process/lifecycle, worker/execution, capture/delivery, resources, registry/diagnostics. Meanwhile the shipped CLI keeps running its own copy of those mechanisms (38 production files frozen), and 37 mechanism test files move into the package. #149 cuts over. CLI invocations and lifecycle controls go through `DaemonClient` and the old CLI mechanism directory is deleted. The package ends with four export paths (root, process-entry, worker-entry, testing) and the freeze test becomes compiler-backed absence/reachability guards. Ten built-CLI status/stop test scenarios are also deleted, with no stated reason.

Boundaries that moved:
1. **Mechanism ownership** (the CLI/package boundary) moves in two steps. #148 duplicates: the body exists in both places and the CLI route stays local. #149 moves callers and deletes the CLI body.
2. **Host vs. mechanism**: invocation classification, workspace discovery, local execution and the backend executor stay host-side. Everything mechanism-like goes behind the client.
3. **Public surface** narrows to a closed four-path export set. Policy serialization goes internal.
4. **Test access** moves from private internals to a read-only inspector. Test actors move to the package side.
5. **Enforcement** moves from "promise" (a freeze on copies) to "wall" (absence plus compiler-backed inventories).

## 5. Questions the top layer left open

1. What is on `DaemonClient`'s surface, and what exactly are "lifecycle controls" (start / stop / status / warm-up?)?
2. At #148, does anything at runtime use the staged package body? Is there a test path that exercises it, or is it dead code until #149?
3. Which 38 files are frozen, and how is "frozen" enforced before it becomes the absence check (hash? file list?)? Why 38 frozen copies but 37 relocated test files: related or coincidence?
4. "Dynamically loads its runtime": how, from where, and what happens when the runtime can't load? Which non-Node host needs Node-free declarations?
5. Who consumed policy serialization and the temporary policy-testing path before removal, and what replaces them?
6. What is "focused composition" concretely: which objects replace `LocalDaemonTransport`, and does behavior change?
7. What are "controlled actors"? What does the executor URL point at, and is it the same as the CLI's "injected backend executor"?
8. What do the compiler-backed inventories check about "clock sources" and "external storage access", and why those two?
9. Does #149 change any user-visible status/stop behavior? The deleted scenarios cover prompt stuck-daemon status, draining in-flight work and force-kill. Did those behaviors survive, move, or go untested?
10. Why were the 10 scenarios built-CLI tests? Was the removal forced by the test-boundary change (no private paths)?
11. What is the "spool", and what are "warm-output cleanup" and "replay safety"?
12. Does serializing daemon test files hide a concurrency problem (shared sockets / state dirs)?
13. How many PRs' worth of change is "compressed into the #147 baseline", and is anything from earlier layers load-bearing here?
